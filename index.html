About Agrisweet

Rooted in Kenya Harvested with care Shared with purpose
At Agrisweet, we cultivate and sell premium Hass avocados, pixie oranges, mangoes and honey with deep care for the soil, the climate, and the people behind every harvest, every orchard, and every jar. From seed to shelf, we honor the land that gives our fruit its honest flavor.

Our products

Honey

Our honey is harvested from honey bees, kept in apiaries situated deep within the Makuli and Nzaui forests. These forests are rich with natural and exotic trees, offering our bees an exceptional blend of nectar sources — the foundation of our honey's distinctive flavor.

Pixies

Our pixie oranges are the true, original variety, grown through a regenerative farming approach that restores the land it grows on rather than depleting it. We deliberately keep pesticide use low, favoring organic production methods over chemical intervention wherever possible.

Mangoes

We cultivate three distinct varieties — Kent, Goa, and Apple — each grown in the best geographical locations under optimum climatic conditions for flavor development. We recognize the risk pesticides pose to biodiversity, so we keep their use to a minimum.

Avocados

We grow and supply premium Hass avocados using sustainable methods that put the environment first. From soil health to water use, every step of our farming process is built to take care of the land that sustains it — protecting biodiversity and reducing our footprint at every stage.
